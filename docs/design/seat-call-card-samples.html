Sample A — the dashboard’s print register

This is a cut-out of the dashboard page at card scale, built to carry the credibility the dashboard itself is built around (HANDOFF decision 7): the green-grey paper ground with the faint press grain, a mono eyebrow and wordmark, one hairline-ruled fact list instead of cards, and the same printed-ink coalition colours. A single seat leaves no room for a hemicycle, so the uncertainty the page encodes as a hollow ring on a 222-seat chamber becomes the hero here: one large hollow ring in the projected coalition’s ink, reading “too close · inside six points”. It states 3.4 points plainly rather than as a claim of precision, exactly as the dashboard’s seat tip would. The caveat stays red in the colophon position — the “Not calibrated” discipline is load-bearing, not decoration. It reads like a report card: credible, formal, authorial, and deliberately un-tagline-like.

Sample B — the register-a content register

This reads as one of the site’s learn pages, not as the dashboard: the same shared tokens, but a border-lean, white-space-led composition that opens on the seat name as a large serif heading with an italic gloss under it, in the register-a idiom. Where Sample A shows the arithmetic as a ruled table, this one catches the reader with a short plain-language sentence and a small, friendly margin bar — the same hollow-ring “too close” signal, but as a dot you can read at a glance rather than a statistic to inspect. The arithmetic framing is kept, in the softer ink-faint voice instead of the red caveat. It reads like an approachable explainer post meant for someone new to the site — warmer and more shareable-feeling, at the cost of some of Sample A’s data-desk authority.
