About MyPolitik

MyPolitik maps every Malaysian Parliament and State (DUN) seat. Tap a state, then a district, to see your representative, party, and GE15 result — built from public, official data, shown transparently.

Sources: DOSM · data.gov.my · electiondata.my (Thevesh) · SPR · Wikipedia / Wikidata · Projection: krackedmaps

Plus datasets we build and curate ourselves — by-election overlays, current affiliations, candidate profiles, live counts — every figure linked to its source.
