THE METHOD, IN THE OPEN

More access.
The same honesty.

Professional tools should make the limits easier to see. The public methodology is the starting point for Analyst.

Read the public methodology ↗
01

The Baseline is a record.

GE15 results are the fixed starting point. A Projection is a modelled estimate of what might change.

02

The assumptions are provisional.

The Swing Model's constants were chosen by judgement. They have not been fitted against survey data.

03

A Seat Call is arithmetic.

Swing is uniform within a state. A Seat Call follows from its GE15 margin, without a Seat-specific signal.
